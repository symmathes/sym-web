What symmathes means

From the Greek syn- (together) and manthánein (to learn) — co-learning.

symmathes is a nightly study companion built for a parent and a middle-schooler to use together, not a tutoring app aimed at the child alone. Each evening it hands the parent a 90-second Brief — the topic, an adult-level explainer, and three questions to ask — so a parent who's forgotten the syllabus can still sit down and be useful. The child plays a short, game-like session that rewards effort and comebacks on questions they missed before, so there's something to win daily, long before the marks catch up.

We're not selling tutoring. We're selling twenty minutes a parent can actually spend well — the competence to be useful tonight, and proof, most weeks, that it made a difference.
